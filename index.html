Head out with your essentials checked.

Reusable checklists for your routine. Quick lists for right now. A little reassurance before you go.

Make every check your own

Keep templates for work, school, the gym or travel. Make an optional item required just for today, and add the things you only need this time.

Remember the way back

Open a completed check and choose Bring these back. Your return check includes only the things you checked on the way out.

A quick list, in a few lines

Paste one item per line for shopping, packing or an order. Save a one-off check as a template whenever you want to reuse it.

On iPhone, with an optional Apple Watch

Check items, use reminders and open checks from widgets or complications. Your paired devices exchange data through Apple's WatchConnectivity. Notification delivery and widget placement depend on your settings and the system.

Takealong Plus

Free to download. Active checking, editing and reminder features require an auto-renewable monthly subscription: US $1.99/month or Japan ¥200/month. Other regional prices are shown before purchase. Eligible new subscribers receive one month free, then the subscription renews automatically unless canceled. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings.

Without an active subscription, you can view saved templates and history, export backups, manage history storage and restore local automatic backups.

Requires iOS 26 or later. The optional Watch app requires watchOS 26 or later. The interface is currently English.

How can we help?

Help with your checks, reminders, backups and subscription.

Contact support

Ask a question or report an issue. Include your iOS/watchOS version, Takealong version, what you expected and what happened.

Issues are public. Please do not post checklist contents, backups, receipts, contact details or other personal information.

Today and Templates

Templates hold the reusable lists you set up for next time. Today shows scheduled and ongoing checks and the checks you finished today. Choose Start another check for a template you use occasionally, or Quick check to paste a one-off list.

Bring your things back

Open a completed check and select Bring these back. This creates a fresh check from the items you actually checked, including optional items and items added just for that outing.

Reminders

Allow notifications in system Settings. Turn Notifications off inside a check to silence that check. Focus modes, notification settings and the system affect delivery. Opening Takealong refreshes its future reminder schedule.

Apple Watch

Keep Bluetooth/Wi-Fi available and open Takealong on both paired devices. Changes synchronize when WatchConnectivity is available. On supported Watches, the system's notification Double Tap action opens the check. Change Time opens a reminder delay picker with the Digital Crown and one-minute +/− controls.

Back up and restore

Changes update an automatic backup stored locally on your iPhone. Export a separate backup to Files or iCloud Drive from Settings. Local automatic backups are not an automatic cloud sync service. Keep a separate exported copy before removing the app or changing devices.

Manage your subscription

Open Takealong Plus and select Restore Purchases if an active subscription is missing. Use Manage Subscription or your Apple Account settings to view renewal details or cancel. Apple handles billing and refunds; Apple billing support can help with charges.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Cancel a free trial before it ends to avoid being charged. Access continues for the applicable paid period after cancellation.

Your data

Takealong does not require an account. Checklist data stays on your devices. See our Privacy Policy for backup retention and deletion details.
